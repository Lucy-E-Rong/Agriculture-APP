Status: implemented and deployed for acceptance; not released or validated

- `REQ-002`: obsolete sample-data notice removed.

## Acceptance still required

The user or their team must verify the working product flow with their own Demo
access and sample image: successful analysis persistence, newest-first Recent
Scans, detail reopening, and expected UX on supported clients. Only after that
acceptance may the Product Manager mark requirements `validated` and treat
`0.2.0` as released.
